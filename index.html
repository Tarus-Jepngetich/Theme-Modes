Lets Explore Var Character

I am making this to explore the CSS Variable var() in styling my workflow. The reason why I am opting to be using this is because it will make it easier for me to update styles across large projects, it will improve the consistency in my work, updating colors will be easier, the cherry on to is that works well in a compnent-based design systems. I recently noticed that its great for theming, hence i have made this mini-project to be about toggling the light and dark theme modes.
